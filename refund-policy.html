If a product isn't right for you, email hello@nodium.co within 14 days of purchase with your order number and we'll issue a full refund — no forms, no interrogation, no hard feelings.

  • Eligible: any product purchased within the last 14 days, provided the files haven't been redistributed or publicly posted.

How to request one: email hello@nodium.co with the subject "Refund", your order number, and one sentence on what went wrong. That's it.
