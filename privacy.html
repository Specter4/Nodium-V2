Privacy Policy.

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data Nodium ("we", "us", "our") collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We keep things simple: we collect the minimum needed to sell and deliver digital products, and we never sell your data.

1. Data we collect

  • Order data. When you buy a product: your name, email address, order contents and payment confirmation. Payment card details are processed by our payment provider and never stored by us.
  • Contact data. When you email us or use the contact form: your name, email address and the contents of your message.
  • Newsletter data. When you subscribe: your email address.
  • Technical data. Basic, anonymized analytics such as pages visited, device type and approximate region, used to improve the site.

2. Why we process it

  • To process and deliver your orders (contract performance);
  • To answer support and licensing questions (legitimate interest);
  • To send the newsletter you subscribed to (consent — you can unsubscribe at any time);
  • To prevent fraud and keep the site secure (legitimate interest).

3. What we don't do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data to anyone.
  • We do not use your data for ad profiling.
  • We do not require an account — purchases are guest checkout only.

4. Sharing

We share data only with the processors required to run the business: payment processing (e.g. Stripe), email delivery for receipts and the newsletter, and website analytics. Each processor is bound by its own data-processing terms and only receives the data it needs.

5. Storage & retention

Data is stored on secure, access-controlled infrastructure. Order records are kept for as long as needed for accounting and support purposes (typically a few years); newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe; contact messages are kept until the matter is resolved.

6. Cookies & local storage

Our site uses local storage in your browser to remember your shopping cart and order confirmations. We do not use advertising cookies. Analytics, where used, are configured to be privacy-respecting and anonymized.

7. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR and similar laws), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict processing. To exercise any of these rights, email hello@nodium.co — we respond within 30 days.

8. Children

Our site and products are not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Security

We use HTTPS everywhere, secure payment processing, and access controls on any systems holding personal data. No method of transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

10. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date above and, where practical, notify subscribers by email.

11. Contact

Privacy questions: hello@nodium.co or via the contact page.
